On a rainy stoop I found *Selective Trout* with the subtitle *A DRAMATICALLY NEW AND SCIENTIFIC APPROACH TO TROUT FISHING ON EASTERN AND WESTERN RIVERS* by Doug Swisher and Carol Richards with a preface by the late great Joe Brooks. I scooped it off that scoop and leafing through it soon broke the spine and so concocted visions of a riparian collages, or something crafty. Meanwhile I'm thinking about "Big Two-Hearted River" and *Trout Fishing in America* and Hem and Richard Brautigan and offing yourself and being found, discovered much like America. And it's sad but it doesn't have much to do with my life except I like seeing the kids biking to Prospect Park Lake with fishing rods. Richard Ford has some good fishing stories, too, in Rock Springs, although I wouldn’t know if it’s trout they’re fishing for. Similar to lures — decoys.
